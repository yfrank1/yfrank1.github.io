About Me

I am a system architect at Huawei Cloud, where I design and evolve large-scale distributed storage systems. My work brings together storage software, networking, and hardware to improve performance, reliability, and resource efficiency.

I also work on storage systems for AI, with a focus on KV cache management and tiering between memory and SSDs, distributed memory pooling, and GPUDirect Storage (GDS). I explore hardware–software co-design to improve data access efficiency for AI training and inference.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University in 2022, under the supervision of Prof. Jiwu Shu and Prof. Youyou Lu.

Current Work

Huawei Cloud · System Architect, Cloud StorageJul. 2022 – Present

  • Cloud storage architecture. I lead architectural design and technical planning for storage pools spanning SSDs, HDDs, and tape, supporting object, block, and file storage services.
  • Storage I/O offloading. I co-design data paths, protocols, and hardware acceleration mechanisms to reduce software overhead and integrate new hardware capabilities into production systems.
  • Memory systems for AI. I develop distributed memory pools and memory–SSD tiering mechanisms for KV caches, focusing on capacity, data access latency, and network efficiency.
  • Technical leadership. I lead a team of 30 engineers, coordinate system architecture and product delivery across teams, and collaborate with university researchers to address practical storage challenges.
