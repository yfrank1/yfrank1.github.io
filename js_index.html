About Me

I am a system architect at Huawei Cloud Storage, where I work on the architecture and evolution of large-scale distributed storage systems. My work connects storage software, networks, and hardware to improve performance, reliability, and resource efficiency.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University in 2022, advised by Prof. Jiwu Shu and Prof. Youyou Lu.

Current Work

Huawei Cloud · System Architect, Cloud StorageJul. 2022 – Present

  • Cloud storage architecture. Lead storage-pool architecture and technical planning across SSDs, HDDs, and tape, supporting object, block, and file storage services.
  • Storage I/O offload. Co-design data paths, protocols, and hardware acceleration to reduce software overhead and bring new hardware capabilities into production systems.
  • Memory systems for AI. Develop distributed memory pools and memory–SSD tiering for KV caches, focusing on capacity, data access latency, and network efficiency.
  • Technical leadership. Lead a team of 30 engineers, coordinate architecture and product delivery across teams, and connect university research with practical storage challenges.
